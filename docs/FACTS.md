## Wording limits

- The film says PUSD is *designed* to hold one dollar. It does not say the value cannot move. The launch post states that
  redemptions were disabled during the initial launch window, so the film does not mention redemption at all.
- The fee claim is about one measured transaction and its price on that day.
- No investment advice, and none of these words: guaranteed, risk-free, always $1, trustless, revolutionary.
